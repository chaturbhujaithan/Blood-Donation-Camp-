Every donation matters

Donated blood supports patients facing surgery, serious illness, injury, and other medical needs. A qualified collection team will guide donors through every step.

About the initiative

Stronger communities start with care.

Our blood donation drive brings eligible donors together with trained medical professionals to help maintain a reliable blood supply. We aim to make donation welcoming, informed, and accessible to everyone who can safely take part.

01

Before you arrive

Check current eligibility guidance, get a good night's rest, and have a meal and water beforehand.

02

At the drive

Staff will explain the process, answer questions, and complete a confidential health screening before donation.

03

After donating

Take time to rest, enjoy refreshments, and follow the care instructions provided by the collection team.
