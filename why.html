Why blood donation matters

Why donate blood?

Blood is essential for patients facing emergencies, surgery, cancer treatment, and many other serious conditions. It cannot be made or replaced, so hospitals depend on voluntary donors.

A single donation can help provide care to people in need. By giving blood, you can make a meaningful difference for patients and families in your community.
